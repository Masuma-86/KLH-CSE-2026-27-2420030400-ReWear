Fast Fashion Reality

The Hidden Cost of Unused Clothing

Traditional consumption patterns produce unsustainable environmental damage

🌊

2,700 Liters Per Shirt

Producing a single cotton t-shirt requires approximately 2,700 liters of water—enough to satisfy one person's drinking water needs for 900 days.

⏳

200+ Years to Decompose

Synthetic fabrics like polyester, nylon, and acrylic are petroleum-derived plastics. When discarded into landfills, they can take over two centuries to break down.

🔥

1 Garbage Truck Per Second

Globally, the equivalent of one full garbage truck of textiles is burned or landfilled every single second. ReWear interrupts this destructive linear flow.

Actionable Tips

Extending the Life of Your Clothes

Small adjustments to everyday laundry and storage double garment lifespans:

❄️ Wash Cold (30°C / 85°F)

Cold water prevents shrinkage, preserves dye brilliance, and cuts 90% of washing machine energy.

🌬️ Air Dry Over Tumble

Tumble dryers degrade fabric fibers and cause lint buildup. Air-drying keeps clothes soft and shape-stable.

🪡 Learn Visible Mending

A missing button or tiny seam split takes 5 minutes to stitch and turns a worn piece into beloved heritage wear.

🔄 Swap Instead of Shopping

Whenever you crave wardrobe novelty, browse ReWear first before purchasing new fast-fashion pieces.
