Sustainable Philosophy

Why Circular Fashion Matters

Over 92 million tons of clothing end up incinerated or in landfills every year. ReWear turns your closet into a sustainable community resource.

📦

1. Declutter Consciously

Take good-condition clothes sitting unworn in your wardrobe and list them in under two minutes with simple phone photos.

🔄

2. Fair Swap or Free Giveaway

Choose whether to trade for another piece you love, or offer it as a gift to students, families, and neighbors who need it.

🌱

3. Track Communal Impact

Every accepted exchange updates item availability and records your verified environmental diversion metric on your profile.
